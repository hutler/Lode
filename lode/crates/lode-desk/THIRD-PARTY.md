# Third-party licences of Lode Desk

Lode Desk is Apache-2.0. Most of what it is built from is under MIT or Apache-2.0, like the
rest of Lode (`cargo tree` lists it all). One part has terms of its own:

## Slint

The window is made with [Slint](https://slint.dev) (crates `slint`, `slint-build` and the
`i-slint-*` crates they pull in), used under the **Slint Royalty-free License 2.0**. That
licence allows desktop applications such as Lode Desk without a fee, on condition that the
application shows that it is made with Slint: the About dialog says "Made with Slint
(slint.dev), used under the Slint Royalty-free License 2.0". Slint's other options are
GPL-3.0, which would not fit an Apache-2.0 program, and a paid licence.

Licence text: https://github.com/slint-ui/slint/blob/master/LICENSES/LicenseRef-Slint-Royalty-free-2.0.md
